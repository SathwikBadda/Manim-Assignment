Step 1: The Rabbit — A Character of Curves

The **Rabbit** is a complex character assembly built primarily from ellipses and circles to create a soft, organic look.

-   **Body & Head**: A large vertical ellipse forms the body, while a circle sits on top for the head.
-   **Ears**: Two long, thin ellipses form the ears. Each ear has an inner pink ellipse (`#FFB6C1`) layered on top of the outer beige ellipse (`#F0E6D2`). They are rotated slightly outwards to give a perked-up appearance.
-   **Face**: The face is detailed with dark eyes, a small pink triangular nose, and whiskers (simple lines). The mouth is formed by two small arcs curving outwards from the center line.
-   **Limbs**: Small ellipses at the base serve as feet, and a fluffy white circle at the back acts as the tail.

The entire assembly is outlined in brown (`#8B7355`) to define the shapes clearly against any background.

Step 2: The Carrot — Texture and Detail

The **Carrot** is designed to complement the rabbit.

-   **Body**: The main body is an orange polygon, tapered to a point at the bottom and wider at the top. Rounded corners soften the geometric feel.
-   **Texture**: Thin, semi-transparent lines traverse the body horizontally, simulating the natural ridges found on a carrot.
-   **Leaves**: The top consists of multiple green ellipses arranged in a fan shape, representing the leafy greens. Darker green strokes add depth.
-   **Highlights**: Two white ellipses with low opacity are placed on the body to create a specular highlight, giving the carrot a fresh, crunchy appearance.
