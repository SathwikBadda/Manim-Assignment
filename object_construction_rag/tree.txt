Step 1: The Seed — Potential in a Small Package

The **Seed** is the starting point. It is a simple, compact shape consisting of a brown oval body (`#8B6F47`). A darker curved line adds surface texture, suggesting a shell or husk. A small, semi-transparent highlight gives it volume. The seed represents the dormant phase of growth.

Step 2: The Sprout — Emergence

The **Sprout** marks the first sign of life. It retains a remnant of the seed casing at the base. From this, a thin, curved green stem (`#7BC043`) rises upward. Two small leaves unfurl from the top, simple ellipses with darker veins. This stage emphasizes vertical growth and fragility.

Step 3: The Plant — Structure and Strength

The **Plant** shows established growth. The stem thickens into a rounded rectangle, providing a sturdy vertical axis. The leaves are larger, more numerous, and arranged in pairs along the stem. They feature distinct light and dark green shades to suggest depth and lighting. The vein detailing on the leaves becomes more pronounced, indicating a maturing vascular system.

Step 4: The Tree — Maturity and Complexity

The **Tree** is the final stage. The trunk is now a thick, tapered solid polygon in dark brown, textured with vertical lines to resemble bark. The canopy is not a single shape but a complex cluster of overlapping circles in varying shades of green (`#5FA52F`, `#7BC043`, `#4A7C2F`). This layering creates a sense of dense foliage. The overall shape suggests a full, healthy tree with a broad crown.
